Bots, AI Agents and What Your Website Needs to Do

Automated visitors are becoming part of the web. Your site should be easy for people to use, clear for search systems to understand and deliberate about which crawlers it welcomes.

How much web traffic is automated?

Reports about automated and AI-agent traffic are changing quickly, and headline percentages vary by provider, definition and measurement method. The reliable takeaway for a small business is not one magic number: automated systems increasingly read, index and act on web content.

Three practical changes

  1. Review robots.txt: decide which useful search and citation crawlers you welcome.
  2. Use clear structure: give every page one clear heading, descriptive links, useful metadata and semantic content.
  3. Protect the human journey: keep forms accessible, explain what happens next and make it easy to contact a person.

Readable is resilient

A fast, well-structured website helps everyone: visitors on phones, assistive technologies, search engines and future AI tools. Do not build a separate machine-only experience before the human experience is clear.

Our starting point

Clear HTML, an honest robots policy, accurate business information and useful pages that answer real questions.
