Ethical AI Agents: Workflows That Respect People

An AI agent is not ethical because it uses the right label. It is ethical when its boundaries, responsibility and effect on people have been considered.

Start with purpose

Before building an agent, define the job it is meant to support. A clear purpose makes it easier to decide what information it needs, what it must not access and when it should hand over to a person.

Four practical boundaries

  • Data: use only the information necessary for the task.
  • Authority: do not let an assistant make commitments it is not authorised to make.
  • Explanation: make it clear when a person is interacting with AI or receiving AI-assisted content.
  • Escalation: define when uncertainty, sensitivity or a complaint requires a human.

Trust is part of performance

A fast answer that creates confusion or hides responsibility is not a successful workflow. Ethical design protects the customer relationship and gives staff confidence about what the system is allowed to do.

Before launch

Write down the purpose, data boundary, human owner, approval point and failure path.
