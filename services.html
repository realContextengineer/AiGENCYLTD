THE AiGENCY PRINCIPLE

Start with the work, not the tool.

If a task is repetitive, rules-based and low-risk, integration may help. If it depends on judgement, trust, safeguarding or a relationship, AI should support the human rather than replace them.

AiGENCY Ltd provides practical AI integration, one-to-one Zoom support, charity AI safety guidance and design support across Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch, Dorset and Hampshire.
