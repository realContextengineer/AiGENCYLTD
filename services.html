What should AI actually do?

Start with the work, not the tool. If a task is repetitive, rules-based and low-risk, automation may help. If it depends on judgement, trust, safeguarding or a relationship, AI should support the human rather than replace them.

That distinction is the centre of our approach: useful automation, human oversight and no pressure to become “an AI person”.
